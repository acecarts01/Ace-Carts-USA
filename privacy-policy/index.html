Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect: your name, email address, shipping address, phone number, and order details. We do not collect payment information — all crypto payments are made directly to wallet addresses, and we do not process credit cards.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your order
  • To send you order confirmations and shipping updates
  • To respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • To comply with legal obligations

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your shipping address with our shipping carriers (USPS, UPS, FedEx) for the purpose of delivering your order. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Age Verification

Our products are intended for adults 21 years of age and older. By using this website, you confirm that you are at least 21 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 21.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. By using our site, you acknowledge and accept this inherent risk.

Cookies and Analytics

This website may use session storage for cart functionality and age gate verification. We may use basic analytics to understand site traffic. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or trackers.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To make such a request, contact us at info@acecarts.org.

Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at info@acecarts.org.
