How to Order

Ordering Ace Ultra Premium Carts is simple. Follow the steps below to go from browsing to delivery.

1

Browse & Select Products

Visit our Shop page and browse all Ace Ultra Premium products. Filter by category — 4G Dual, Diamond Sauce, Mini Gen 4, 510 Liquid Diamond, Gen 4 Flavors, Wholesale, or New Flavors. Click any product to open the quick-view for a full description and add it to your cart.

♠ Tip: Use the filter tabs to narrow down by product type or category
2

Check Minimum Order ($100 USD)

All orders must meet the $100 USD minimum. This applies to both retail and wholesale orders. If your cart falls below $100, you'll see a notice and won't be able to proceed to checkout until the minimum is met. For wholesale master box pricing, visit our Wholesale page.

♠ Wholesale orders: contact us for custom pricing on large volumes
3

Choose Your Payment Method

We accept multiple payment methods. Cryptocurrency is our preferred method — we accept BTC, ETH, USDT (ERC-20 and TRC-20), SOL, XRP, and BNB. Wallet addresses and QR codes are provided at checkout. You can also place orders directly via WhatsApp. Apple Pay, Chime, and PayPal availability — please confirm with us before use as availability may vary.

♠ Use code ACECRYPTO1 at checkout for 5% off your first crypto order
4

Complete Checkout

Enter your shipping address and contact details. For crypto payments: send the exact amount shown to the wallet address provided, and include your order number in the memo/note field if your wallet supports it. For WhatsApp orders: you'll be redirected to a pre-filled WhatsApp message — just send it and we'll confirm your order within 24 hours.

5

Order Confirmation

After your payment is received and verified, you'll receive an order confirmation email from info@acecarts.org and a WhatsApp or Telegram update within 24 hours. If you don't receive a confirmation, contact us directly at info@acecarts.org.

6

Shipped from Los Angeles

All orders are packed and dispatched from our Los Angeles, California warehouse within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation. A tracking number is sent by email and WhatsApp. US delivery is typically 3–7 business days. International orders are available — contact us for rates and estimated delivery times.

📦 Free shipping on orders over $250 USD within the USA
Need Help Ordering?

Our team is available 7 days a week via WhatsApp and Telegram to assist with orders, wholesale inquiries, and product questions.
