Where Do Orders Ship From?

All orders ship from our warehouse in Los Angeles, California. This means fast US delivery and reliable processing for international orders.

Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation. Weekend and holiday orders are processed on the next business day.

US Delivery Timelines

  • Standard: 3–7 business days
  • Expedited: 1–3 business days (additional cost — contact us)
  • Free shipping on US orders over $250 USD

International Shipping

We ship internationally. Estimated delivery: 7–21 business days. Additional customs and import fees may apply — buyer is responsible. Contact us before ordering internationally for current rates.

Discreet Packaging

Every order ships in plain, unmarked packaging. No brand logos or product descriptions on the outside. Return address uses a neutral sender name.

Tracking

Tracking numbers are sent by email (info@acecarts.org) and WhatsApp within 24 hours of shipment. See the full Shipping Policy and Refund Policy. For questions, contact us.
