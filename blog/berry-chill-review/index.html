Berry Chill by Ace Ultra Premium:
The Ultimate Flavor Review

📅 May 2025·7 min read·Flavor Review

Berry Chill is one of the most ordered flavors in the Ace Ultra Premium Gen 4 lineup, and for good reason. This complete review covers the taste profile, aroma, effects, device quality, and how to add it to your next order from acecarts.org.

What Is Berry Chill?

Berry Chill is a 2G Gen 4 disposable from Ace Ultra Premium. The official flavor and aroma profile reads: Strawberry · Ice · Berries. Effects: Relaxed, Inspired, Peaceful — a balanced hybrid experience suited for afternoon and evening use. Adults 21+ only.

Taste Profile

The first thing you notice is a clean, strong menthol rush on the inhale — cool and sharp without being artificial. On the exhale, a clear mixed berry blend comes through — strawberry up front, with dark berry (blackberry, blueberry) underneath. The menthol finish lingers. It is refreshing, fruity, and one of the most consistent flavor profiles in the Gen 4 lineup.

Berry Chill becomes a go-to — especially in warm months when you want something cool, fruity, and genuinely well-made.

Aroma

Light, pleasant mixed berry with a subtle cool note. Vapor is smooth and carries the berry sweetness accurately. It is not overpowering, which makes it easy to use throughout the day.

Device Quality

Berry Chill comes in the standard Ace Ultra Premium 2G form factor — sleek, gold-accented build with the Ace of Spades logo. Draw is smooth and consistent from first puff to last. Battery life outlasts the oil under normal use. If you want to compare sizes, read our collection comparison.
