Top 10 Ace Ultra Premium Flavors
Ranked 2025

📅 May 2025·6 min read·Flavor Review

With 130+ flavors across 7 collections, choosing from the Ace Ultra Premium catalog can feel overwhelming. This ranking is based on order frequency, community feedback, and overall flavor experience for 2025.

1. Tiger's Blood — Gen 4

The most reordered flavor in the entire lineup. Tiger's Blood delivers a bold tropical blend of strawberry, watermelon, and coconut. Sweet, layered, and deeply satisfying. Shop Disposable Vapes.

2. Berry Chill — Gen 4

Cool mixed berry with a strong icy finish. Strawberry leads, dark berries follow. Read the complete Berry Chill review.

3. Watermelon Mint — Gen 4

Crisp watermelon and a clean mint finish. One of the most universally loved refreshing flavors in the Gen 4 lineup.

4. Lunar Lychee Ice — Gen 4

Exotic lychee with a strong icy cooldown. One of the newest Gen 4 additions and quickly one of the most reordered.

5. Dragon Fruit Bliss — Love Edition

Rich tropical hybrid from the Love Edition. Deep terpene-forward flavor that rewards slow, measured draws.

6. Peach Rings — Love Edition (Sativa)

Bright, sweet peach sativa. One of the top-selling Love Edition flavors for its uplifting effect and accurate peach candy flavor.

7. Rainbow Sherbet — Spring Edition (Sativa)

Vibrant multi-fruit sherbet with an uplifting sativa experience. A standout from the new Spring Edition.

8. Banana Taffy — Signature Collection

Sweet banana candy from the Signature Collection. Smooth, unique, and highly reorderable.

9. Strawberry Dream — Black Badge (Sativa)

Fresh sweet strawberry in the premium Black Badge Collection format.

10. Jolly Rancher — 4G Gen 2 (Sativa)

Hard candy multi-fruit blast in the extended 4G format. See the 4G Gen 2 complete review.
